🎮 How to Play Prayag

✌️

1. Two-Hand Pinch to Capture

Bring thumb & index finger together on BOTH hands at the same time. A 3-second countdown will start and snap your photo!

🤏

2. One-Hand Pinch to Solve Puzzle

Pinch ONE hand over a floating tile to grab it. Drag it over its matching slot and release to snap it into place!

📥

3. Download Your Strips

Once solved or anytime in the sidebar gallery, download your high-resolution photo strips or clear them to start a fresh collection.
